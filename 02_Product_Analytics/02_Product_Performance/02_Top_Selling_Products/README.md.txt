# Top Selling Products

## Business Objective

Identify products with the highest sales volume to understand customer purchasing behavior and demand.

---

## Key Insights

- Low-priced accessories dominate unit sales despite contributing relatively less revenue.
- Stickers, pens, notebooks and mugs consistently appear among the highest-selling products.
- Customers frequently purchase inexpensive products, indicating strong impulse-buying behavior.
- High-volume products create opportunities for cross-selling with premium merchandise.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Recommend accessories during apparel purchases to increase Average Order Value.
- Bundle frequently purchased low-cost items together.
- Place high-volume products near checkout to encourage impulse purchases.
- Use best-selling accessories as promotional products during campaigns.
