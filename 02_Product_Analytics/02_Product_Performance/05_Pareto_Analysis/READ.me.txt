# Pareto Analysis (80/20 Rule)

## Business Question

Do a small percentage of products contribute the majority of business revenue?

---

## Business Importance

Pareto Analysis helps businesses identify high-impact products and prioritize inventory, marketing, and merchandising efforts.

---

## Grain

Product Level

---

## KPIs

- Product Revenue
- Revenue Contribution %
- Cumulative Revenue %

---

## SQL Approach

Calculated product revenue, revenue contribution, and cumulative revenue using window functions.



---

## Key Insights

- Revenue is concentrated among a relatively small proportion of products.
- The cumulative revenue curve demonstrates diminishing returns as lower-performing products are added.
- Focusing business efforts on top-performing products can produce greater revenue impact than treating every product equally.
- Pareto analysis helps prioritize merchandising, inventory management and marketing investments.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory availability for top-performing products.
- Allocate marketing spend towards products driving the majority of revenue.
- Periodically review long-tail products for optimization opportunities.
- Develop premium product recommendation strategies around high-contributing products.---

## Interview Discussion

This analysis combines aggregation with cumulative window functions to identify revenue concentration across the product catalog.
