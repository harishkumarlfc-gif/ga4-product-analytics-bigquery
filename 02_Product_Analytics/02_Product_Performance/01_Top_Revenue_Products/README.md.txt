# Top Revenue Products

## Business Question

Which products generated the highest revenue?

---

## Why does this matter?

Identifying high-revenue products helps businesses prioritize inventory, promotions, and merchandising strategies.

---

## Grain

Product Level

---

## KPI

**Total Revenue = SUM(item_revenue)**

---

## SQL Approach

Aggregated item-level revenue from the validated Product Master dataset and ranked products by total revenue.

---



## Business Insights

- Apparel products dominate the top revenue rankings, indicating that higher-priced merchandise contributes significantly to total revenue.
- Google Zip Hoodie F/C generated the highest revenue ($10,488), followed by Google Crewneck Sweatshirt Navy ($9,878).
- Multiple hoodie, sweatshirt and fleece products appear within the top rankings, suggesting strong customer demand for premium apparel.
- Accessories such as bottles and backpacks also appear among the highest revenue products, demonstrating opportunities for complementary product recommendations.


---
## Business Recommendations

- Increase visibility of high-performing apparel through homepage promotions.
- Cross-sell inexpensive accessories with premium apparel to increase basket value.
- Ensure sufficient inventory for top revenue-generating products.
- Feature high-performing products during seasonal campaigns.
---

## Interview Discussion

Revenue identifies products generating the greatest business value. It differs from units sold because premium products may generate high revenue despite lower sales volumes.
