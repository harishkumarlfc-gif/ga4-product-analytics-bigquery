# Product Performance Analytics

## Objective

Analyze product-level performance to identify high-performing, low-performing, and revenue-driving products using the validated Product Master dataset.

---

## Dataset

Google Merchandise Store (GA4 BigQuery Public Dataset)

---

## Analytical Dataset

Product Master

---

## Analyses Included

- Top Revenue Products
- Top Selling Products
- Product Revenue Contribution
- Product Ranking
- Pareto Analysis
- High Revenue Low Volume Products
- Low Revenue High Volume Products
- Product Performance Matrix

---

## Business Value

These analyses help product managers understand which products drive revenue, attract demand, and deserve further investment or optimization.
