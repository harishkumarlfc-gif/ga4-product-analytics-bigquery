# High Revenue Low Volume Products

## Business Question

Which products generate high revenue while selling relatively few units?

---

## Business Importance

These products often represent premium offerings that contribute significant revenue without requiring high sales volumes.

---

## Grain

Product Level

---

## KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Units Sold
- Revenue per Unit

---

## SQL Approach

Aggregated revenue and quantity at the product level and calculated revenue generated per unit sold.

---

## Key Insights

- Premium apparel generates significantly higher revenue per unit than most other products.
- Backpacks and premium jackets achieve high revenue despite comparatively fewer purchases.
- High revenue per unit suggests customers are willing to spend more on premium merchandise.
- These products represent strong upselling opportunities.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Increase visibility of premium products during customer journeys.
- Recommend premium products alongside frequently viewed items.
- Highlight premium products within recommendation engines.
- Consider premium product bundles to increase Average Order Value.
## Interview Discussion

Revenue alone cannot identify premium products. Combining revenue with units sold provides a clearer view of product value and pricing position.
