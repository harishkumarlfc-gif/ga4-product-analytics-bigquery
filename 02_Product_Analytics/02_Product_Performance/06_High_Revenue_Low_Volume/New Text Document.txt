# High Revenue Low Volume Products

## Business Question

Which products generate high revenue while selling relatively few units?

---

## Business Importance

These products often represent premium offerings that contribute significant revenue without requiring high sales volumes.

---

## Grain

Product Level

---

## KPIs

- Total Revenue
- Units Sold
- Revenue per Unit

---

## SQL Approach

Aggregated revenue and quantity at the product level and calculated revenue generated per unit sold.
