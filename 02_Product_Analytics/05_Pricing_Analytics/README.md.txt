# 💰 Pricing Analytics

## Overview

Pricing Analytics evaluates how product pricing influences customer purchasing behavior and overall business performance.

This module analyzes Average Selling Price (ASP), product performance across different price ranges, and compares budget and premium product segments to identify pricing opportunities that support revenue growth.

---

# Business Objectives

- Measure the Average Selling Price (ASP).
- Analyze product performance across different price ranges.
- Compare budget and premium product segments.
- Understand how pricing influences sales volume and revenue.
- Support pricing strategy and merchandising decisions.

---

# Analyses Included

| Analysis | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Average Selling Price | Measures the average revenue generated per unit sold. |
| Price Buckets | Evaluates product performance across predefined price ranges. |
| Premium vs Budget | Compares revenue and sales performance between pricing segments. |

---

# Business Value

Pricing Analytics helps businesses optimize pricing strategies by understanding how customers respond to different price points.

Key business applications include:

- Improving Average Order Value (AOV).
- Optimizing product assortment.
- Supporting pricing decisions.
- Identifying premium product opportunities.
- Improving merchandising strategies.
- Increasing revenue through better pricing.

---

# Key Insights

- The overall Average Selling Price is **$19.52**.
- Products priced between **$10–$24.99** generate the highest revenue.
- Budget products drive sales volume, while premium products contribute significantly to revenue.
- A balanced product portfolio across multiple pricing segments supports sustainable business growth.
