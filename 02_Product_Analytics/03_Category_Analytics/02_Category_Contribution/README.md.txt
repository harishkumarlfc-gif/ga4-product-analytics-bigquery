# Category Revenue Contribution

## Business Question

How much does each product category contribute to total business revenue?

---

## Business Importance

Revenue contribution identifies the categories that have the greatest impact on overall business performance. It helps prioritize investments, inventory planning, and marketing efforts.

---

## Grain

Category Level

---

## KPIs

- Category Revenue
- Revenue Contribution (%)

---

## SQL Approach

Calculated total revenue for each category and used a window function to compute each category's percentage contribution to overall revenue.

## Product Manager Takeaway

A small number of categories often contribute a large share of revenue. These categories should receive higher strategic focus while lower-contributing categories can be evaluated for optimization or expansion opportunities.

---

## Interview Discussion

Contribution analysis is more informative than absolute revenue because it normalizes category performance and highlights each category's relative importance to the business.
