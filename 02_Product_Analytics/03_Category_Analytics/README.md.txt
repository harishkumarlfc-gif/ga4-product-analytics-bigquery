# 📂 Category Analytics

## Overview

Category Analytics evaluates product performance at the category level to understand how different product segments contribute to overall business performance.

This analysis identifies the highest revenue-generating categories, measures their contribution to total revenue, tracks category rankings, and monitors revenue growth over time. These insights support merchandising, inventory planning, pricing strategy, and marketing decisions.

---

# Business Objectives

- Identify the highest revenue-generating product categories.
- Measure each category's contribution to total revenue.
- Rank categories based on business performance.
- Analyze category-level revenue trends over time.
- Support strategic merchandising and inventory planning.

---

# Analyses Included

| Analysis | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Category Revenue | Identifies the revenue generated by each product category. |
| Category Revenue Contribution | Measures each category's percentage contribution to total revenue. |
| Category Ranking | Ranks categories based on revenue performance. |
| Category Growth | Tracks Month-over-Month (MoM) revenue growth for each category. |

---

# Business Value

Category-level analysis enables businesses to understand where revenue is generated and prioritize investment accordingly.

Key business applications include:

- Identifying high-performing product categories.
- Optimizing inventory allocation.
- Improving merchandising strategies.
- Detecting seasonal demand patterns.
- Supporting marketing budget allocation.
- Monitoring category performance over time.

---

# Key Insights

- Apparel is the largest revenue-generating category, contributing nearly half of total revenue.
- Revenue is concentrated among a relatively small number of categories, with Apparel significantly outperforming the rest.
- Most categories experienced strong growth during December, indicating seasonal purchasing behavior.
- Category rankings provide a clear view of strategic revenue drivers and opportunities for optimization.
