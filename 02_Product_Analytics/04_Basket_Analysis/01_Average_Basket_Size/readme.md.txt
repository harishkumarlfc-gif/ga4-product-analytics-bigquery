# Average Basket Size

## Business Question

What is the average revenue generated per customer order?

---

## Business Importance

Average Basket Size (Average Order Value) is a key ecommerce KPI used to measure customer spending behavior. It helps evaluate pricing strategies, promotions, and cross-selling initiatives.

---

## Grain

Order Level

---

## KPIs

- Total Orders
- Total Revenue
- Average Basket Size

---

## SQL Approach

1. Aggregated product revenue at the transaction level.
2. Calculated total revenue for each order.
3. Computed the average order revenue across all transactions.

## Product Manager Takeaway

Increasing Average Basket Size is one of the fastest ways to grow revenue without acquiring additional customers. Bundling products, personalized recommendations, and upselling are common strategies to improve this metric.

---

## Interview Discussion

Because the source dataset is at the product level, revenue was first aggregated to the order level before calculating the average basket size. This prevents underestimating customer spend.
