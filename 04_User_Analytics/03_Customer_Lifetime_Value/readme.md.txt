# 04 Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

## Business Question

Which customers generate the highest lifetime value for the business?

---

## Business Importance

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) measures the total revenue generated by a customer over their purchasing history.

Rather than treating every customer equally, CLV helps product and business teams identify high-value customers, prioritize retention efforts, and design personalized experiences that maximize long-term revenue.

---

## Data Grain

Customer

Each row represents one unique `user_pseudo_id`.

---

## KPI

- Total Orders
- Lifetime Revenue
- Average Order Value (AOV)

---

## Metric Definitions

### Total Orders

Number of valid completed purchases made by a customer.

Only purchases with:

- `event_name = 'purchase'`
- `transaction_id IS NOT NULL`
- `transaction_id != '(not set)'`

are included.

---

### Lifetime Revenue

Total revenue generated by a customer across all completed purchases.

```sql
SUM(item_revenue)
```

---

### Average Order Value (AOV)

Average revenue generated per completed order.

```text
Lifetime Revenue / Total Orders
```

---

## SQL Approach

- Filter valid purchase events.
- Exclude purchases with missing or invalid transaction IDs.
- Aggregate purchase history at the customer level.
- Calculate total orders, lifetime revenue, and average order value.
- Rank customers by lifetime revenue.

## Business Insights

- The highest-value customer generated **1,530** in lifetime revenue from a single purchase, demonstrating that large one-time purchases can contribute significantly to overall revenue.
- Several customers achieved high lifetime revenue through **repeat purchases**, with some placing **10–13 orders**, highlighting the importance of customer retention.
- High purchase frequency does not always translate into the highest revenue, as some repeat customers have relatively low average order values while others generate substantial revenue through fewer, higher-value purchases.
- Customer value varies considerably across the user base, indicating that different retention and engagement strategies may be appropriate for different customer segments.

---

## Product Manager Takeaway

Customer Lifetime Value shifts the focus from acquiring new users to maximizing the value of existing customers.

By identifying high-value and repeat customers, product teams can design loyalty programs, personalized recommendations, and targeted retention campaigns that improve long-term revenue while reducing customer acquisition costs.
