# 🚀 Case Study 02 — Increasing Average Order Value Through Product Strategy

## Business Scenario

Following the successful optimization of checkout conversion, the Product Manager wants to identify the next opportunity to increase revenue.

The objective is to grow revenue **without significantly increasing marketing spend or website traffic**.

Using insights from the Product Analytics dashboard, this case study focuses on increasing **Average Order Value (AOV)** through product recommendations and cross-selling.

---

# Step 1 — Business Objective

Revenue can be increased by:

- Acquiring more customers
- Increasing conversion rate
- Improving customer retention
- Increasing Average Order Value (AOV)

Since customer acquisition is often expensive, increasing AOV represents an efficient way to grow revenue using existing traffic.

---

# Step 2 — Dashboard Insights

Product Analytics revealed several important observations.

### Observation 1

Customers purchase an average of:

**4.38 products per order**

This indicates that customers are already comfortable purchasing multiple products within a single transaction.

---

### Observation 2

Top-selling products are primarily low-priced accessories.

Examples include:

- Stickers
- Pens
- Notebooks

These products generate high sales volume but relatively lower revenue.

---

### Observation 3

Apparel products generate the highest revenue.

Examples include:

- Google Zip Hoodie
- Crewneck Sweatshirt
- Heavyweight Pullover
- Tech Fleece

Although apparel sells fewer units than accessories, each purchase contributes significantly more revenue.

---

# Business Opportunity

Customers are already purchasing multiple products.

Instead of encouraging customers to buy **more items**, the opportunity is to encourage customers to purchase **higher-value combinations of products**.

This creates an opportunity for intelligent cross-selling.

---

# Proposed Strategy

Recommend inexpensive accessories alongside high-value apparel.

Example recommendations:

Google Zip Hoodie

↓

Customers also bought

- Google Sticker
- Google Pen
- Notebook Set

The additional cost is relatively small compared to the primary purchase, making impulse purchases more likely.

---

# Why This Strategy?

This recommendation is supported by the dashboard.

- Apparel generates high revenue.
- Accessories generate high sales volume.
- Customers already purchase multiple items per order.

Rather than increasing traffic, the strategy focuses on increasing the value of existing customer baskets.

---

# Experiment Design

## Hypothesis

Displaying relevant accessory recommendations alongside apparel products will increase basket value by encouraging impulse purchases.

---

## Experiment

A/B test personalized cross-sell recommendations on:

- Product Detail Pages
- Shopping Cart
- Checkout

Variant A

Current experience

Variant B

Personalized accessory recommendations

---

# Success Metrics

## Primary Metric

- Average Order Value (AOV)

---

## Guardrail Metrics

- Checkout Conversion Rate
- Revenue per Visitor

---

## Supporting Metrics

- Accessory Attachment Rate
- Apparel Revenue
- Products per Order

---

# Expected Business Impact

If successful, this strategy could:

- Increase Average Order Value
- Increase accessory sales
- Improve overall revenue
- Generate additional revenue without increasing customer acquisition costs

---

# Key Learning

Product Analytics is not only about identifying high-performing products.

It is also about understanding how products interact within customer baskets and using those insights to design strategies that improve business performance.

Rather than recommending generic product bundles, this analysis uses purchasing behavior and product performance data to propose targeted cross-selling opportunities supported by dashboard insights.
