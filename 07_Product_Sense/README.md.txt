# 🧠 Product Sense

## Overview

Technical analysis identifies **what happened**.

Product Sense focuses on **why it might have happened**, **what business opportunity exists**, and **which experiments should be prioritized** to improve product performance.

Using insights generated from the Google Merchandise Store dataset, this section translates SQL analysis and dashboard findings into product decisions that could be evaluated by a Product Management team.

> **Note:** The public GA4 dataset provides behavioral data but does not contain sufficient information to establish causality. Therefore, observations are separated from hypotheses, and all recommendations are presented as experiments rather than conclusions.

---

# Product Sense Framework

Every recommendation in this project follows the same framework.

```

Observation
        ↓
Business Impact
        ↓
Hypothesis
        ↓
Experiment
        ↓
Primary Metric
        ↓
Guardrail Metric
        ↓
Expected Business Outcome

```

This approach ensures recommendations are driven by data while acknowledging the limitations of the available dataset.

# Goal

The objective is not to prove why customers behave in a certain way.

Instead, the objective is to:

- Identify opportunities
- Generate hypotheses
- Prioritize business problems
- Design measurable experiments
- Recommend metrics for success

This mirrors how Product Analysts support Product Managers in real-world product development.
