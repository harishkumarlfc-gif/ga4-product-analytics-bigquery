# 📈 KPI Tree

## Overview

A KPI Tree breaks down a high-level business objective into measurable drivers that influence business performance.

Rather than optimizing a single metric in isolation, Product Analysts identify the underlying factors that contribute to business growth and prioritize improvements at each level.

For an ecommerce business, **Revenue** is one of the most important business outcomes.

Instead of treating revenue as a standalone metric, it can be decomposed into smaller, actionable drivers.

---

# Revenue KPI Tree

```

Revenue
│
├── Orders
│   ├── Traffic
│   └── Conversion Rate
│       ├── View Item → Add to Cart
│       ├── Add to Cart → Begin Checkout
│       └── Begin Checkout → Purchase
│
└── Average Order Value (AOV)
├── Products per Order
└── Average Selling Price (ASP)

```

---

# Understanding Each Driver

## Revenue

Revenue is the primary business outcome for this project.

It can increase in two ways:

- Increase the number of completed orders.
- Increase the average value of each order.

---

## Orders

Orders represent successful purchases.

Order growth depends on two major drivers:

### Traffic

More qualified visitors create more purchasing opportunities.

Potential initiatives:

- Marketing campaigns
- SEO
- Paid acquisition
- Referral programs

---

### Conversion Rate

Not every visitor completes a purchase.

Improving conversion allows the business to generate more orders without increasing traffic.

The purchase funnel used in this project:

```

View Item

↓

Add to Cart

↓

Begin Checkout

↓

Purchase

```

Each stage provides an opportunity to identify user friction and optimize the customer journey.

---

## Average Order Value (AOV)

Average Order Value measures the average amount spent per completed order.

Increasing AOV allows revenue to grow without acquiring additional customers.

AOV can be improved through two primary levers.

---

### Products per Order

Customers purchasing multiple products increase basket value.

Possible product initiatives include:

- Product bundles
- Cross-selling
- Personalized recommendations
- Frequently bought together suggestions

---

### Average Selling Price (ASP)

Increasing the average value of products purchased also increases AOV.

Potential initiatives include:

- Premium product recommendations
- Upselling
- Higher-value product placement
- Pricing strategy optimization

---

# Business Takeaways

Based on the dashboard analysis, revenue growth can be achieved through multiple independent levers.

Priority opportunities identified during this project include:

- Reducing checkout abandonment to improve completed orders.
- Increasing AOV through cross-selling and product recommendations.
- Improving customer retention to increase repeat purchases.
- Optimizing high-performing products to maximize revenue contribution.

---

# Key Learning

A KPI Tree helps Product Analysts understand how individual product metrics contribute to overall business performance.

Rather than asking:

> "How do we increase revenue?"

Product teams ask:

- Which driver has the largest opportunity?
- Which improvement is easiest to implement?
- Which initiative is expected to create the highest business impact?

Breaking business goals into measurable components enables data-driven prioritization and experimentation.
