## Data Quality Notes

During data validation, two important observations were made:

1. Some purchase events contained duplicate transaction-item combinations.
2. Some purchase events had missing transaction IDs (`(not set)`).

For this project:

- Duplicate transaction-item pairs were investigated and deduplicated based on documented assumptions.
- Purchase events with missing transaction IDs were retained for product-level analysis but excluded from transaction-level KPIs such as Order Count and Average Order Value.

All assumptions are documented in the Data Validation module.
