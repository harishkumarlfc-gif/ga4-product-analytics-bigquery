# Funnel Analytics

## Overview

Funnel Analytics evaluates how users progress through the ecommerce purchase journey and identifies where potential customers abandon the conversion process.

This module analyzes user movement across key funnel stages, measures conversion performance, quantifies funnel leakage, and compares conversion behavior across devices and traffic sources to uncover opportunities for improving customer acquisition and purchase conversion.

---

# Business Objectives

- Understand customer progression through the purchase funnel.
- Measure user conversion at each stage of the buying journey.
- Identify where the highest customer drop-offs occur.
- Evaluate conversion performance across different funnel stages.
- Compare conversion behavior by device and traffic source.
- Support Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) through data-driven insights.

---

# Analyses Included

| Analysis | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Event Funnel | Tracks user progression across key ecommerce events from product view to purchase. |
| Product Funnel | Evaluates product-level customer engagement throughout the purchase journey. |
| Funnel Leakage | Measures customer drop-off between each stage of the funnel. |
| Stage Conversion | Calculates conversion rates between consecutive funnel stages. |
| Conversion by Device | Compares funnel conversion performance across desktop, mobile, and tablet users. |
| Conversion by Traffic Source | Evaluates how different acquisition channels perform throughout the conversion funnel. |

---

# Business Value

Funnel Analytics helps businesses understand where potential customers leave the purchase journey and prioritize the highest-impact optimization opportunities.

Key business applications include:

- Improving purchase conversion rates.
- Reducing checkout abandonment.
- Identifying friction within the customer journey.
- Optimizing product discovery and checkout experience.
- Evaluating acquisition channel quality.
- Supporting product and UX optimization initiatives.
