# Growth Analytics

## Overview

Growth Analytics focuses on understanding where business growth originates and identifying opportunities to acquire, engage, and retain customers.

Unlike Product Analytics, which evaluates product performance, and User Analytics, which focuses on customer behavior, Growth Analytics examines acquisition channels, devices, geographic markets, and business trends over time.

This module uses the Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Public Ecommerce Dataset to analyze key growth drivers.

---

## Data Source

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Public Ecommerce Dataset

---

## Analyses Included

### 1. Traffic Source

Evaluate customer acquisition channels.

Key metrics include:

- Revenue
- Orders
- Conversion
- Top Acquisition Sources

---

### 2. Device Analysis

Compare business performance across desktop, mobile, and tablet devices.

Key metrics include:

- Revenue
- Orders
- Users
- Device Contribution

---

### 3. Geographic Analysis

Analyze customer distribution across countries.

Key metrics include:

- Revenue
- Orders
- Users
- Geographic Contribution

---

### 4. Time Series Analysis

Track business performance over time.

Key metrics include:

- Daily Revenue
- Daily Orders
- Daily Users
- Revenue Trend

---

## Business Value

These analyses help answer questions such as:

- Which acquisition channels drive business growth?
- Which devices generate the highest revenue?
- Which markets should the business prioritize?
- How is business performance changing over time?
