# Retention Analytics

## Overview

Retention Analytics measures how effectively an ecommerce business retains customers over time.

While acquisition drives new customers, long-term business growth depends on encouraging customers to return and make repeat purchases.

This module analyzes repeat purchasing behavior, customer cohorts, retention rates, and churn using the Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Public Ecommerce Dataset.

---

## Data Source

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Public Ecommerce Dataset

---

## Analyses Included

### 1. Repeat Purchases

Measure how many customers make multiple purchases.

Key metrics include:

- One-time Buyers
- Repeat Buyers
- Repeat Purchase Rate

---

### 2. Cohort Analysis

Group customers by their first purchase month and analyze purchasing behavior over time.

Key metrics include:

- Cohort Size
- Returning Customers
- Cohort Retention

---

### 3. Retention Analysis

Measure how many customers return after their first purchase.

Key metrics include:

- Retained Customers
- Retention Rate

---

### 4. Churn Analysis

Measure customers who never returned after their first purchase.

Key metrics include:

- Churned Customers
- Churn Rate

---

## Business Value

These analyses help answer questions such as:

- How many customers become repeat buyers?
- How effectively does the business retain customers?
- Which customer cohorts perform best?
- What percentage of customers are lost after their first purchase?
